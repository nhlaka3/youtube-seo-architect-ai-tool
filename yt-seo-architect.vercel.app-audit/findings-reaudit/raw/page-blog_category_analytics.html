📊 Analytics & Metrics

CTR, retention, impressions, and analytics metrics explained

7 guides
← All Categories

YouTube analytics tells you exactly what's working and what isn't — if you know how to read it. Most creators check views and subscribers, then miss the metrics that actually drive growth: click-through rate (CTR), average view duration, impressions, and audience retention curves. This category decodes all of it.

CTR tells you if your title and thumbnail are competitive in the search results and suggested feed. Retention tells you if your content delivers on its promise. Impressions tell you whether YouTube is even testing your videos with new audiences. Each metric points at a different fix, and these guides map metric to action.

You'll learn which numbers matter at which channel size, how to interpret a retention graph that drops at 30 seconds vs 2 minutes, and what analytics patterns reliably predict a video about to take off.
