## CORE FEATURES ($10 Standard Edition)

### 1. Keyword Discovery Engine
**What it does:** Implements the Alphabet Loop and Asterisk Wildcard search techniques to discover untapped keywords that your competitors aren't targeting.

**How it works:**
- Enter a seed keyword (e.g., "crypto")
- System runs A-Z autocomplete loop (crypto + a, crypto + b, crypto + c...)
- Also tests asterisk wildcards (crypto * near me, crypto * 2026, etc.)
- Discovers Golden Keywords with high intent + low competition

**Why buyers love it:** Finds keyword opportunities you'd never think of manually. It's like having a keyword research team working 24/7.

---

### 2. Channel & Video Auditing
**What it does:** Deep audit of any YouTube channel or individual video against strict 2026 algorithmic baselines.

**Audit checks:**
- Title keyword front-loading
- Description word count (>200 words)
- Tag optimization (≤15 terms)
- Timestamp presence in descriptions
- Thumbnail click-through potential
- Retention metrics analysis
- Accessibility (captions/transcripts)
- Niche relevance scoring
- Playlist structure optimization
- Session watch time optimization

**Why buyers love it:** Shows exactly what's wrong and what to fix. No more guessing.

---

### 3. Metadata Auditor
**What it does:** Scans individual videos and provides pass/fail checklist with auto-fix capability.

**Checks performed:**
- Title optimization (keyword front-load, <60 chars)
- Description quality (200+ words)
- Tag relevance (≤15 optimized tags)
- Timestamp placement
- Category alignment

**Auto-fix feature:** One-click "Magic Fix" button that generates AI-optimized metadata and applies it directly to YouTube.

---

### 4. Thumbnail Heuristics Analyzer
**What it does:** Analyzes thumbnails for algorithmic effectiveness using computer vision.

**Analysis includes:**
- Face detection (faces in thumbnails get 38% more clicks)
- Text overlay detection
- Color contrast scoring
- Emotional impact measurement
- "Part X/Y" badge generation for series content
- Custom badge overlay with drag-and-drop preview

**Outputs:** Badge images ready to upload to YouTube Studio.

---

### 5. Retention Re-Orderer
**What it does:** Analyzes viewer drop-off points in your playlist and reorders videos automatically to maximize session watch time.

**How it works:**
- Fetches retention data via YouTube API
- Identifies "gateway" videos (high retention entry points)
- Identifies "leaky" videos (viewers stop watching there)
- Auto-reorders playlist putting highest retention videos first

**Why buyers love it:** Proven to increase average watch time by 15-40%.

---

### 6. Playlist Growth Suite
**What it does:** Complete toolkit to turn playlists into "suggested video" magnets.

**Module: Metadata Weaver**
- Generates 300-word descriptions optimized for algorithm
- Creates "collusion tags" that signal related content
- Woven tags boost suggested video appearances

**Module: Session-Start Linker**
- Generates special URLs that start playback at optimal points
- Links directly to your gateway video
- Maximizes first-5-second engagement

**Module: Gateway Identifier**
- Detects videos with highest "playlist start" rates
- Identifies which videos convert browse to watch

---

### 7. Script-to-Shorts Detector
**What it does:** Analyzes long-form transcripts and identifies high-retention "hooks" suitable for Shorts conversion.

**How it works:**
- Scans video transcripts for attention spikes
- Identifies "WOW moments" viewers re-watch
- Suggests timestamps to clip for Shorts
- Auto-links related Shorts to long-form source

---

### 8. Niche-Relevance Guard
**What it does:** Validates that your metadata matches your niche to avoid algorithm penalty.

**Validation checks:**
- Compares your tags to top competitors
- Scores niche relevance (0-100)
- Warns if you're targeting wrong keywords
- Suggests alternative relevant tags

**Why buyers love it:** Prevents the #1 mistake that kills channel growth: targeting keywords outside your niche.

**What it does:** Uses Groq AI to generate optimized titles, descriptions, and tags.

**Generates:**

- Tag bundles
- Series abstracts

**Included in Standard:** Limited to 500 AI requests/day.

**What it does:** Real-time health monitoring of all features.
