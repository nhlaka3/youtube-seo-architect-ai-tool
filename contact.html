Frequently Asked Questions

Is YT SEO Architect really free?

Yes. All 17 tools are free with no credit limits. We monetize through optional premium features and blog content, not by gating the core tools.

Do you store my YouTube data?

We use OAuth 2.0 for authentication and never store your YouTube credentials. We only access the data needed for the specific tool you're using, and you can revoke access at any time.

How do I report a bug?

Use the form above, email us directly, or open an issue on GitHub. Include what you were doing, what happened, and your browser/OS if relevant.

Can I use this for my agency or team?

Absolutely. The tool works with any YouTube channel you have access to. Manage client channels, optimize multiple series, and run audits across your entire portfolio.

When will new tools be added?

We ship new features regularly. Check the changelog for the latest updates, or subscribe to our blog for announcements.
