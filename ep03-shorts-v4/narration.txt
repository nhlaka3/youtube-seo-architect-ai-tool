Stop optimizing videos like singles. YouTube ranks playlists as a session, not individual uploads. And most creators have this completely backwards. Here is the playbook. Step one, order by intent flow. Put the search-ready topic first. Then chain logical subtopics that make viewers want to keep watching. This one change alone can double your playlist session depth. Step two, name your playlist with target keywords. Write a description that previews what comes next. This tells YouTube exactly how your series connects. Most creators skip this and wonder why their playlists never surface in search. Step three, enable autoplay and reduce drop-off. Sequence your episodes so each one naturally leads to the next.
