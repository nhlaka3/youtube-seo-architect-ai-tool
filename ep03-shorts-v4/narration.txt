Stop optimizing videos like singles. YouTube ranks playlists as a session, not individual uploads. Here is the playbook.

Step one, order by intent flow. Put the search-ready topic first. Then chain logical subtopics that make viewers want to keep watching.

Step two, name your playlist with target keywords. Write a description that previews what comes next. This tells YouTube exactly how your series connects.

Step three, enable autoplay and reduce drop-off. Sequence your episodes so each one naturally leads to the next. This boosts session depth fast.
