How to Use the Keyword Difficulty Scorer (Find Easy-to-Rank Keywords)

Learn how to use the Keyword Difficulty Scorer to find low-competition keywords your channel can rank for.

What Is the Keyword Difficulty Scorer?

The Keyword Difficulty Scorer analyzes how hard it would be to rank for a specific YouTube keyword based on competition metrics. It considers channel authority of current top-ranking videos, optimized title/tag usage, view counts, and engagement rates of competitors.

Step 1: Enter a Keyword Idea

Type a keyword or phrase you want to rank for. The tool works best with specific long-tail keywords (4-6 words) rather than broad short-tail terms. A keyword like "how to edit YouTube videos for beginners" will score differently than "video editing."

Step 2: Review the Difficulty Score

Keywords score from 0-100. 0-30: Easy to rank (ideal for new channels). 30-60: Medium difficulty (good for growing channels). 60-100: Highly competitive (established channels only). The tool also shows why the keyword scored what it did.

Step 3: Compare Multiple Keywords

Enter several keyword ideas for the same video topic. Compare their difficulty scores side by side. Pick the keyword with the best balance of search volume (shown via volume indicator) and low difficulty score.

Step 4: Create Content Around Your Chosen Keyword

Once you've selected a target keyword, use the Title Optimizer and Description Writer to create optimized content around it. Target 1 primary keyword and 2-3 secondary keywords per video.

Pro Tips for Keyword Research

Target keywords with difficulty under 40 for channels under 1,000 subscribers. Mix 1 competitive keyword (to dream) with 2-3 easy keywords (to win) per video. Re-check keyword difficulty monthly — it changes as competitors publish new content.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good keyword difficulty score?

For new channels, target scores under 30. For established channels (1K-10K subs), target under 50. For large channels, scores up to 70 are feasible. Above 70 is typically only achievable by top-tier channels.

How does the difficulty score work?

The score analyzes the top 10 ranking videos for your keyword, checking: average channel subscriber count, view counts, title/tag optimization level, description quality, and engagement rates. Higher competition in these areas = higher difficulty score.

Can I rank for high-difficulty keywords as a small channel?

Yes, by targeting long-tail variations. Instead of "YouTube SEO," target "YouTube SEO for small channels 2026" — much lower difficulty, same audience intent.
