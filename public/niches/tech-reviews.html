📋 Tech Review Channels: SEO Strategies That Work

Use Product Name + Keyword Format

Start titles with the exact product name followed by the review type: "iPhone 16 Pro Review: 6 Months Later" or "MacBook Air M4 vs MacBook Pro M4 — Which Should You Buy?"

Create Comparison and Versus Content

Comparison videos ("X vs Y") consistently outperform single-product reviews. They capture viewers who are deciding between products — high-intent traffic.

Include Price and Value Signals in Titles

Include pricing context: "Best Budget Laptop Under $800," "Is the Pro Worth $400 More?" These titles attract viewers in the decision phase.

Benchmark Results Drive Authority

Include benchmark scores, battery test results, and performance metrics. Create dedicated timestamp sections for each benchmark.

Update Old Reviews with New Context

Revisit popular reviews after 6-12 months with updated context: "iPhone 16 Pro Review — Still Worth It in 2026?" YouTube rewards fresh content.

Use High-Contrast Product Thumbnails

Tech thumbnails perform best with the product clearly visible against a contrasting background. Bright, clean lighting signals production quality.
