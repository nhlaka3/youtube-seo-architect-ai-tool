📋 Business & Marketing Channels: SEO Strategies That Work

Target Problem-Solution Keywords

Target problem-solution keywords: "how to increase website traffic," "best email marketing tool." Your title should promise a specific outcome.

Include Numbers and Data in Titles

Business audiences respond to data-driven titles: "5 Ways to Increase ROI," "3 Marketing Tools That Saved Us $50K/year." Numbers signal concrete, actionable content.

Use Case Studies and Real Examples

Business content with real case studies, before/after metrics, and specific examples outperforms theoretical advice.

Optimize for B2B and B2C Keywords Separately

B2B keywords: "enterprise software," "SaaS metrics." B2C keywords: "side hustle," "freelance tips." Create separate content strategies for each.

Leverage LinkedIn Cross-Promotion

Cross-promote your videos on LinkedIn with a text summary and link. This drives external traffic and builds your professional audience simultaneously.

Create Tool Tutorials and Comparisons

Business viewers actively search for tool tutorials: "How to Use HubSpot CRM," "Notion vs Asana." These have high commercial intent.
