5 YouTube Shorts Mistakes That Kill Your Growth

YouTube Shorts can be a powerful tool for growing your channel, but making certain mistakes can hurt your chances of success. Here are 5 common mistakes to avoid:

Incorrect Video Format

What: Uploading videos that don't meet YouTube's Shorts requirements (60 seconds or less, vertical format). Why it hurts: YouTube's algorithm favors content that meets its requirements, so incorrect formatting can lead to reduced visibility. How to fix: Ensure your videos are 60 seconds or less and in a vertical format (9:16 aspect ratio) to increase their chances of being discovered.

Poor Titles and Tags

What: Using vague or irrelevant titles and tags. Why it hurts: YouTube's algorithm relies on titles and tags to understand your content, so poor choices can make it hard for viewers to find your videos. How to fix: Use specific, descriptive titles and tags that accurately reflect your content, and make sure to include relevant keywords.

Low-Quality Thumbnails

What: Using low-quality or unattractive thumbnails. Why it hurts: Thumbnails are crucial for grabbing viewers' attention, and low-quality ones can deter clicks. How to fix: Create high-quality, eye-catching thumbnails that accurately represent your content and make viewers want to click.

How to fix: Respond to comments and engage with your audience to build a loyal following and increase your video's visibility.
