What You Need Before Starting

Before you begin diagnosing YouTube video view drops, ensure you have the following prerequisites in place:

  • YouTube Account Requirements:
    • Creator account with a verified email address.
    • Access to YouTube Analytics for your channel.
  • Tools Needed:
    • Google Chrome browser or a similar desktop browser.
    • YT SEO Architect (free AI-powered YouTube SEO tool) for data analysis and insights.
  • Technical Skills:
    • Basic understanding of YouTube Analytics and its various reports.
    • Comfortable with browser navigation and basic HTML.
  • Estimated Time:
    • 1-2 hours for setting up and understanding the prerequisites.
    • Additional time for analyzing your video and implementing changes.

Step 1: Set Up YouTube Analytics

Begin by logging into your YouTube account and navigating to the YouTube Studio. From the left-hand menu, select "Analytics" and then "View" to access your video view data. Ensure that the time range is set to "All time" or a relevant period for your analysis. You should see a graph displaying the total views for your video over time.

⚠️ DON'T SKIP: Verify that your YouTube Analytics account is properly linked to your YouTube channel to ensure accurate view data.

By setting up YouTube Analytics, you'll be able to track changes in your video's view count over time, helping you identify potential issues that may be contributing to the view drop.

Step 2: Identify Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Next, identify the key performance indicators (KPIs) that are most relevant to your video's performance. For this analysis, focus on the "views" and "average view duration" metrics. You can also consider other KPIs like "watch time" and "audience retention" to gain a more comprehensive understanding of your video's performance.

⚠️ DON'T SKIP: Make sure to exclude any irrelevant or anomalous data points that may skew your analysis.

By identifying your KPIs, you'll be able to pinpoint specific areas of your video's performance that may be contributing to the view drop, allowing you to develop targeted strategies for improvement.

Step 3: Examine Video Engagement and Audience Retention

Now, examine your video's engagement and audience retention metrics to see if there are any patterns or areas of concern. Look for sudden drops in engagement or retention, as these may indicate issues with the video's content, pacing, or overall structure.

⚠️ DON'T SKIP: Pay close attention to any anomalies or irregularities in your video's engagement and audience retention patterns, as these may be indicative of a more serious issue.

By analyzing your video's engagement and audience retention, you'll be able to gain a deeper understanding of how viewers are interacting with your content and identify potential areas for improvement.

Step 5: Review Your Thumbnail and Title

Now that you've checked your video's engagement and audience retention, it's time to examine your thumbnail and title. A misleading or unappealing thumbnail can lead to low click-through rates and, subsequently, a drop in views. Ensure your title accurately reflects the content of your video, and your thumbnail is eye-catching and relevant.

Many creators make the mistake of using clickbait titles or misleading thumbnails to attract viewers. However, this tactic can backfire and result in a decrease in views. Be honest and transparent with your audience, and focus on creating high-quality content that resonates with them.

Use YT SEO Architect to analyze your thumbnail and title's performance. This tool can help you identify areas for improvement and provide actionable insights to boost your click-through rates.

Step 7: Optimization

Once you've identified the cause of your video view drop, it's time to optimize your video for better performance. This involves making changes to your title, description, tags, and thumbnail to better match what your target audience is searching for.

Here are some tips to help you optimize your video:

  • Make sure your title accurately reflects the content of your video.
  • Use eye-catching and relevant images in your thumbnail.
  • Tag your video with relevant keywords to help it appear in search results.
