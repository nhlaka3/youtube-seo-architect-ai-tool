YouTube Shorts

Vertical short-form videos (up to 60 seconds) that compete with TikTok and Instagram Reels, with unique algorithm and monetization rules.

What Is YouTube Shorts?

YouTube Shorts is YouTube's short-form video format, featuring vertical 9:16 aspect ratio videos up to 60 seconds long. Shorts have their own algorithm feed (separate from traditional YouTube), their own monetization path (10 million Shorts views in 90 days for YPP eligibility), and their own analytics section in YouTube Studio. Shorts get massive reach potential — a single Short can get millions of views in days — but they monetize at lower rates than long-form content (typically $0.01-$0.03 RPM vs $1-$5 for long-form). The Shorts algorithm prioritizes swipe-through rate (what % of viewers swipe to the next Short) and loop rate (what % rewatch the Short). Successful Shorts hook viewers in the first 2 seconds, use trending audio, and include clear CTAs to long-form content.
