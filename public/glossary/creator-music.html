YouTube Creator Music

YouTube's library of licensed music that creators can use in videos without copyright claims, with revenue-sharing options.

📖 All Terms 📊 Analytics 🤖 Algorithm 🔍 SEO 💰 Monetization 📝 Content Strategy ⚙️ Features

What Is YouTube Creator Music?

Creator Music is YouTube's solution to the copyright problem creators face when using popular songs. It offers two options: buy a license upfront (pay for the song, keep 100% of video revenue) or revenue share (use the song free, share video revenue with the rights holder). Creator Music is available to YouTube Partner Program members in select countries. It covers thousands of popular songs across genres. For non-YPP creators, YouTube's Audio Library offers free music and sound effects that don't require licensing. Always check the specific terms for each song — some may have restrictions on video type or geographic availability.
