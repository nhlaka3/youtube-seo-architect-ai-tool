Shorts Monetization

The specific requirements and revenue rules for earning money from YouTube Shorts, including the Shorts Fund and ad revenue sharing.

📖 All Terms 📊 Analytics 🤖 Algorithm 🔍 SEO 💰 Monetization 📝 Content Strategy ⚙️ Features

What Is Shorts Monetization?

Shorts monetization works differently from long-form YouTube monetization. Creators need 1,000 subscribers AND 10 million Shorts views in the past 90 days to join YPP for Shorts. Once in YPP, Shorts revenue comes from ads shown between Shorts in the Shorts feed, not from ads on the Short itself. The Shorts revenue is pooled with other Shorts creators and distributed based on your share of total Shorts views. Monetization rates for Shorts are significantly lower than long-form content — expect $0.01-$0.05 RPM compared to $1-$5+ for long-form. However, Shorts can be highly effective as a funnel to drive viewers to your monetized long-form content through channel branding and CTAs.

Why Shorts Monetization Matters for YouTube SEO

Shorts Monetization plays a direct role in how much revenue your YouTube channel generates. Whether you're just starting out or already monetized, understanding this aspect of YouTube's monetization system helps you maximize earnings from every view. Channels that optimize for shorts monetization earn significantly more per view than those that ignore it — sometimes 3-5x more. This isn't about gaming the system; it's about understanding how YouTube's revenue mechanics work so you can structure your content and strategy accordingly. Every creator should understand shorts monetization before worrying about view counts.

How to Optimize for Shorts Monetization

To maximize shorts monetization, first ensure you understand the current YouTube monetization policies as they change frequently. Check YouTube Studio's monetization tab for your current status and any policy warnings. Structure your content strategy to align with monetization requirements — create videos over 8 minutes to enable mid-roll ads, target topics with higher CPM potential, and build audience in high-CPM countries. Diversify your revenue beyond ads with channel memberships, Super Chat, and affiliate marketing. Track shorts monetization monthly in YouTube Analytics and compare year-over-year to spot trends. Connect with other monetized creators to share strategies for maximizing revenue.
