¿Qué es Etiquetas de YouTube en YouTube?
Etiquetas de YouTube es palabras clave agregadas a la metadata del video para ayudar a YouTube a entender el contenido y el contexto del video para la búsqueda y las recomendaciones.

¿Qué Es Etiquetas de YouTube?

YouTube tags are descriptive keywords placed in the video's advanced settings that help YouTube's algorithm understand what your video is about. While tags are less important than they were a decade ago (YouTube now uses NLP to analyze transcripts and audio), they still help with keyword matching, especially for new videos before enough watch time data exists. Best practices: use 5-15 relevant tags, start with your primary keyword, include variations and related terms, add common misspellings, and use a mix of broad and specific tags. Avoid keyword stuffing or irrelevant tags — YouTube's system can detect tag spam and it may hurt your rankings. Use a YouTube tag generator tool to find the best tags for your content.
