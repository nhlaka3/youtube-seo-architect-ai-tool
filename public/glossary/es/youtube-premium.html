YouTube Premium

¿Qué es YouTube Premium en YouTube?
YouTube Premium es una suscripción de pago que elimina anuncios, permite reproducción de fondo y proporciona acceso a YouTube Music Premium.

¿Qué Es YouTube Premium?

YouTube Premium is a subscription service ($13.99/month in the US) that removes all ads from YouTube videos, enables background playback (audio continues when you switch apps or lock your phone), grants access to YouTube Music Premium, and allows video downloads for offline viewing. For creators, YouTube Premium revenue is distributed to creators based on how much Premium members watch their content — this can account for 10-25% of a channel's total YouTube revenue. YouTube Premium revenue often has higher effective CPM than advertising revenue. Premium members also tend to watch more content and have higher engagement rates, making them valuable to creators even beyond the direct revenue share.
