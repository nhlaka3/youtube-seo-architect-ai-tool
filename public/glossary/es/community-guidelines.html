¿Qué es Community Guidelines en YouTube?
Community Guidelines es las directrices de la comunidad de YouTube, que prohíben contenido dañino como el acoso, la desinformación, la violencia y el spam. Las violaciones pueden resultar en la eliminación del contenido o la terminación del canal.

¿Qué Es Community Guidelines?

Community Guidelines are YouTube's rules for what's not allowed on the platform. Major categories: spam and deceptive practices (misleading metadata, fake engagement, impersonation), sensitive content (hate speech, harassment, cyberbullying), violent or dangerous content (threats, harmful acts, regulated goods), and misinformation (medical misinformation, manipulated media, election integrity). First violation = warning (no penalty), subsequent violations = strikes (1 strike = 1-week restrictions, 3 strikes in 90 days = channel termination). Community Guidelines are separate from copyright rules — you can get a strike for violating guidelines even with original content. Know the rules for your niche, especially if covering sensitive topics.
