¿Qué es A/B Testing (Thumbnail Testing) en YouTube?
A/B Testing (Thumbnail Testing) es prueba de múltiples versiones de una miniatura de video mostrando diferentes variantes a segmentos de tu audiencia y comparando el rendimiento de CTR.

¿Qué Es A/B Testing (Thumbnail Testing)?

A/B testing on YouTube lets you test up to 3 thumbnail variations for a single video. YouTube shows different variants to different viewers and reports which gets the highest CTR after a statistically significant sample. To run an effective test: create 3 distinctly different thumbnail designs (different colors, layouts, or focal points), test for 2-4 weeks or until a clear winner emerges, apply the winning thumbnail to all viewers, and document what made it work for future reference. Thumbnail testing is one of the highest-ROI activities for YouTube growth — improving CTR from 4% to 6% effectively gives you 50% more views from the same impressions.
