¿Qué es Cost Per Mille (CPM) en YouTube?
Cost Per Mille (CPM) es la cantidad que los anunciantes pagan por cada 1.000 impresiones de anuncios en tus videos. Ganas el 55% de esta cantidad después de la comisión de YouTube.

¿Qué Es Cost Per Mille (CPM)?

CPM is the advertiser-facing metric — it's what brands pay YouTube for 1,000 ad impressions on your content. Your share is typically 55% of the net CPM (after YouTube's 45% revenue share). CPM rates vary dramatically by niche: finance and business content can command $20-$50 CPM, while gaming and entertainment often see $1-$5 CPM. Seasonal factors also affect CPM — Q4 (October-December) sees the highest rates due to holiday advertising. CPM only counts ad impressions, not total views. A video with 100,000 views might generate $200-$500 in ad revenue depending on CPM, viewer location, and how many viewers watch enough to see ads.
