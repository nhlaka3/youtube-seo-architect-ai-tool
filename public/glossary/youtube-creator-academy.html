YouTube Creator Academy

What Is YouTube Creator Academy?

YouTube Creator Academy is YouTube's official learning platform at creatoracademy.youtube.com. It offers free courses covering: channel strategy and growth, content creation and production, community building and engagement, monetization and revenue optimization, and policy and copyright. Each course includes video lessons, reading materials, and quizzes. Completing courses can earn you a Creator Academy certificate. The content is created by YouTube's own education team and features case studies from successful creators. For new creators, the foundational certification courses are an excellent starting point. For experienced creators, the advanced courses on audience retention analysis and monetization optimization offer practical insights. The Academy is updated annually to reflect algorithm and policy changes.
