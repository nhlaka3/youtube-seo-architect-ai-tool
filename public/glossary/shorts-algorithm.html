Shorts Algorithm

The separate recommendation system for Shorts that prioritizes swipe-through rate, loop rate, and first-2-second retention.

📖 All Terms 📊 Analytics 🤖 Algorithm 🔍 SEO 💰 Monetization 📝 Content Strategy ⚙️ Features

What Is Shorts Algorithm?

The Shorts algorithm operates independently from the long-form YouTube algorithm. Key signals: swipe-away rate (what % of viewers swipe to the next Short instead of watching), loop rate (what % of viewers rewatch the Short), completion rate (what % watch to the end), first-2-second hold rate (critical — most swipes happen immediately), and new viewer rate (can the Short bring new people to YouTube). The Shorts algorithm is more aggressive in showing content from unknown creators — it's easier to get your first 10,000 Shorts views than your first 1,000 long-form views. However, Shorts viewers are less likely to subscribe or watch long-form content. Use Shorts for discovery, not monetization.

Shorts Algorithm is one of the key signals the YouTube algorithm evaluates when deciding which videos to recommend and rank.
