YouTube Chapters Template: The Timestamp Format Viewers Love

⚙️ Video Feature Templates · Demand-validated · Updated 2026

Chapters turn one long video into a menu — viewers jump to what they need, watch more, and YouTube rewards the session time. The format is strict; here's the template.

📋 The Copy-Paste Template

SectionCopy-paste template
Chapter 100:00 Intro — what the video covers + why it matters
Chapter 201:30 {Step or section name} — lead with a verb or outcome
Chapter 304:15 {Step or section name}
Chapter 407:00 {Step or section name}
Chapter 509:30 Results / Recap / Q&A
Format rulesMM:SS or H:MM:SS, first chapter must be 00:00, at least 3 chapters for the feature to appear

🧠 When & How to Use It

Put timestamps in the description's first lines or use YouTube's built-in chapters editor. Name chapters with the keyword + outcome, not vague labels.

🎯 Niche Variations

  • Tutorials: chapters = the numbered steps.
  • Reviews: chapters = specs, test results, verdict.
  • Podcasts: chapters = topics with guest names.

🚫 Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • No 00:00 chapter (feature won't activate)
  • Vague names like 'Part 1' instead of outcomes
  • Timestamps that don't match the edited video
  • Fewer than 3 chapters

❓ FAQ

Do YouTube chapters help SEO?

Indirectly — they improve session time and let your sections appear in Google search results as key moments.

How many chapters should a video have?

3-10. Enough to make sections skimmable, few enough to stay meaningful.

What's the exact timestamp format?

MM:SS (e.g., 04:15) or H:MM:SS for videos over an hour, with the first chapter always 00:00.
