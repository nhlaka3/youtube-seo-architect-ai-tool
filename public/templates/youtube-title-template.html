YouTube Title Template: 8 Formulas That Lift Click-Through Rate

🎯 Title Templates · Demand-validated · Updated 2026

Your title decides whether the algorithm tests your video — and CTR is the first test. These 8 formulas cover every hook type, each with a fill-in structure and example.

📋 The Copy-Paste Template

SectionCopy-paste template
Numbers formulaHow I {achievement} in {number} {timeframe} — {lesson}
Curiosity formulaWhy {common belief} is wrong (and what to do instead)
Problem-solution formulaFix {specific problem} in {timeframe} — {no-nonsense solution}
Listicle formula{Number} {topic} Tips That Actually Work in 2026
Question formula{Question the viewer is already asking}? ({number} answers)
Comparison formula{A} vs {B} in 2026: Which One Should You Use?
Story formulaI {action} for {timeframe} — here's what {happened}
How-to formulaHow to {outcome} ({number} step framework)

🧠 When & How to Use It

Pick the formula that matches your video's strongest angle. Put the keyword at the front. Keep under 60 characters (Google truncates ~60-70 in results). Test 2-3 variants with YouTube's Test & Compare.

🎯 Niche Variations

  • Tutorials: use How-to or Numbers formulas — explicit outcomes win.
  • Gaming: use Story or Curiosity — hype + specific patch details.
  • Finance/business: use Problem-solution or Comparison — specificity builds trust.

🚫 Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Clickbait mismatch (title promises what the video doesn't deliver — kills retention)
  • Keywords buried at the end
  • Titles over 60 characters truncating mid-phrase
  • No differentiation from competitors ranking for the same query

❓ FAQ

How long should a YouTube title be?

Under 60 characters. Google shows roughly 60-70 before truncation, and mobile shows even less.

Do titles affect ranking or just CTR?

Both. YouTube uses title keywords for ranking, and CTR determines whether the algorithm keeps showing your video.

Should I A/B test titles?

Yes — YouTube's Test & Compare lets you upload 2-3 titles and shows the winner on real impressions.
