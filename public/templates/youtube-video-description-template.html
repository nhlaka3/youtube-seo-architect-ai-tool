YouTube Video Description Template: The 6-Section Format That Earns Views

📝 Description Templates · Demand-validated · Updated 2026

Most creators write descriptions backward — they start with links and bury the searchable text. This template forces the keyword-rich content first, then supports it with structure.

📋 The Copy-Paste Template

SectionCopy-paste template
Section 1 — Keyword hook{Primary keyword}: {what viewer learns}. {Result} in {timeframe}.
Section 2 — ContextIn this video you'll see {outline of what happens}, including {unique angle}.
Section 3 — Timestamps00:00 Intro
01:30 {step 1}
04:00 {step 2}
07:45 {results}
Section 4 — Resources📌 Tools used: {link}
📌 Free guide: {link}
Section 5 — CTASubscribe for {cadence} {niche} videos. Comment your {question} below.
Section 6 — Hashtags#{niche} #youtube #tutorial

🧠 When & How to Use It

Fill section 1 first — it's what search and the 'more' button show. Keep sections 2-5 skimmable with line breaks. Update timestamps after publishing if you re-cut the video.

🎯 Niche Variations

  • Review videos: lead with the verdict + rating, add specs table, link the product.
  • News/updates: lead with the date + headline, add sources, pin the most relevant comment.
  • Podcasts: lead with guest name + episode topic, add episode timestamps, link audio platforms.

🚫 Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Starting with 'Hey guys welcome back' (wastes the indexed first line)
  • No line breaks (mobile shows a wall of text)
  • Links with no context (viewers don't know what they'll get)
  • Missing the keyword in the first 100 characters

❓ FAQ

What's the ideal YouTube description length?

150-500 words is the sweet spot — long enough for keywords and context, short enough that viewers actually read it.

Do timestamps in descriptions help SEO?

Yes — they create chapter markers that improve session time and let Google surface specific parts of your video.

Where should I put affiliate links in a description?

Below the timestamps and after the keyword-rich content, so the searchable text stays at the top.
