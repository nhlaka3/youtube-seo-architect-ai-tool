YouTube SEO Tool FAQ

Does this YouTube SEO tool violate YouTube's Terms of Service?

No. We use the official YouTube API with OAuth — the same system vidIQ, TubeBuddy, and every other YouTube SEO tool uses. All API calls respect YouTube's quota limits. We don't use bots, scrapers, or fake engagement.

Do I need to give you my YouTube password?

Never. You sign in through Google's official OAuth popup. We receive a token that lets us access your YouTube data. We never see your password. Revoke access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Why not just use ChatGPT?

ChatGPT doesn't connect to your YouTube channel. It can't read your video data, audit your metadata, or apply changes through the YouTube API. YT SEO Architect is built specifically for YouTube creators — it fetches real data from your channel and lets you act on it with one click. And it's 100% free.

How does the YouTube tag generator work?

Our AI YouTube tag generator analyzes your video topic and niche, cross-references YouTube auto-suggest data, and generates a ranked list of optimized tags with competition scores. Try the free tag generator →

Is there really no catch?

No catch. YT SEO Architect is completely free — no subscriptions, no credit card required, no hidden fees. We monetize through partnerships, not your wallet.

What data do you store?

We store your channel ID and optimization history so the dashboard remembers your work. We don't store your videos, comments, or personal information beyond what's needed to run the tools. Your Google access token stays in your browser's local storage.

How do credits work?

Every user gets unlimited AI credits. We've removed all paywalls and limits so every creator can optimize their channel without restriction.

Will this actually improve my YouTube rankings?

Yes — when used consistently. Creators who optimize titles, tags, and descriptions see 30-50% higher CTR and better search rankings within 2-4 weeks. Our Optimization Trials feature tracks your real before/after metrics so you can see exactly what's working.

How does this compare to vidIQ and TubeBuddy?

We offer the same core features — keyword research, tag generation, metadata audits, competitor analysis — completely free. No tiers, no limits. Plus you get AI Coach, Growth Engine, and Script-to-Shorts which aren't available in basic vidIQ/TubeBuddy plans.
