Pricing FAQ

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free plan gives you 100 credits every month — no payment required. Upgrade to Pro or Agency when you're ready for more power.

What happens when I run out of credits?

Your credits reset at the start of each calendar month. Free users get 100, Pro users get 1,000, and Agency users have unlimited credits. You can also upgrade mid-month to get immediate access to more credits.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. All plans are month-to-month through PayPal. Cancel anytime from your PayPal account and you'll keep access until the end of your billing period. No cancellation fees, no contracts.

Can I switch between plans?

Absolutely. Upgrade from Free to Pro or Pro to Agency anytime — your new credits are available immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept PayPal for all paid plans. PayPal supports credit cards, debit cards, and bank transfers. Your payment is processed securely through PayPal — we never see your card details.

Is there a discount for annual billing?

Not yet — we're keeping pricing simple with month-to-month plans. Annual billing with a discount is on our roadmap. Follow our blog for updates.

What can I actually do with 100 free credits?

A lot. You can run 33 AI title generations, 20 keyword research sessions, 10 full SEO audits, or 10 SEO bundles per month — all for free. Most creators find 100 credits is enough for 3-5 videos of full optimization per month.

How quickly will I see results after optimizing?

Most creators see YouTube ranking improvements within 1-2 weeks after applying optimizations. CTR improvements are often immediate. Our Optimization Trials feature tracks your before/after metrics so you can measure exactly what changed and by how much.
