Pricing questions

Is Free really free forever?

Yes. The Free plan does not expire and does not require a card. Pro only removes the daily limits and unlocks automation.

How does payment work?

Checkout runs through PayPal. We never see or store your card details. You can cancel from your PayPal account at any time.

What happens after I pay?

PayPal sends a receipt to your email. Reply with it (or forward it to the contact address) and Pro is activated on your channel within 24 hours.

Can I cancel later?

Yes. Cancel anytime and you stay Pro until the end of the period you paid for, then drop back to Free.

Do the free tools use my YouTube data?

Only if you connect a channel with Google sign-in. Tools work without login; connecting is optional and used solely to run audits.

Why is there a paid tier now?

Hosting and AI inference cost real money. The Free plan covers the workflows most creators need. Pro covers creators who want automation — bulk metadata overhauls, auto-replies, and unlimited AI coaching. Nothing that is free today becomes paid later.

What does "unlimited" mean on Pro?

No daily caps on keyword discovery, audits, or AI coaching. Fair-use limits exist only to prevent automated abuse of the API — normal creator usage never hits them.
